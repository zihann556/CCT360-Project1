A Concert Day with Friends

Concert Vlog — Case Study

Project Overview

This vlog documents a concert day with my friends, from preparing for the event to arriving at the venue and enjoying the performance together.

I wanted to capture both the concert atmosphere and the small moments that made the day memorable.

Creative Process

Planning

I planned the key moments I wanted to capture before the concert.

Filming

I recorded short clips throughout the day to document the atmosphere and moments with friends.

Editing

I selected the best clips, added music and transitions, and arranged them into a smooth vlog.

Results & Takeaways

This project helped me improve my filming, editing, and storytelling skills while creating a more consistent vlog style.
